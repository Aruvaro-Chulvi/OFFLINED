# ✅ Requirements

Offlined is designed to run **fully offline**, even on a modest computer without a dedicated graphics card. These are the **technical requirements**:

### Hardware (recommended)
- **CPU:** x86_64 with **AVX2** support (for good AI performance).
- **RAM:** 8 GB minimum (16 GB recommended for larger AI models and smoother multitasking).
- **Disk:** from 8 to 200 GB, depending on the AI models, Wikipedia (ZIM) files and maps you keep.
- **GPU:** *not required*. If you have an NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics card, Offlined uses it automatically to speed up the AI.

### Operating Systems
- **Windows 10/11 x64**
- **macOS** (coming soon)
- **Linux** (coming soon)

### Storage
- **You can run it from a folder on your PC's drive, from an external hard drive or even from a USB stick.**
- **We recommend a fast external SSD (USB 3.2), as big as you want.**

## 🌍 Who is Offlined for?

**Offlined** is designed for people and organizations that need reliable access to knowledge and tools without depending on a continuous internet connection, including:

- **Privacy-conscious users** who prefer fully local computation, storage and control over their data.
- **Students, schools, libraries and educational programs** in regions where connectivity is poor or expensive.
- **Researchers, archivists and educators** who value long-term, self-hosted access to reference material.
- **Preparedness and outdoor communities** looking for dependable offline information and tools.

These groups are the core use cases today, but Offlined is for anyone who values **resilient access to knowledge**, **digital autonomy** and **offline-first workflows** in an increasingly cloud-dependent world.

## 🙋 FAQ

### Q1. Do I need an internet connection to use Offlined?
No. Everything runs locally: the AI, the study agents, maps, Wikipedia and your document library.
You only need an internet connection to download the content you choose (AI models, Wikipedia ZIM files, maps).

---

### Q2. Can I use Offlined in portable mode (no installation)?
Yes. Offlined is fully portable.
Just launch the `.exe` file: the app runs from a local folder, an external drive or a USB stick.

---

### Q3. Does my data ever leave my computer, external drive or USB stick?
No. Offlined does not send your data anywhere.
There is no cloud backend, no account, no telemetry and no analytics. Offlined only connects to the internet when you ask it to download content, and the in-app Privacy report lists every connection.

---

### Q4. Does Offlined send my chats to Microsoft, Google, OpenAI or any other company?
No.
The AI runs on your own computer. Your conversations are never sent to external services.

---

### Q5. How much disk space does Offlined need?
It depends on what you choose to download:

- Base application → ~2 GB (including the sample content)
- One AI model → from ~3 GB to ~17 GB, depending on the model
- Wikipedia in your language → from a few GB (mini version) to 40–120 GB (full version with images)
- Maps → from a few MB for a small country to ~140 GB for the whole world at maximum detail
- Your own documents and media → up to you

You decide how big your offline world is.

---

### Q6. Does the app use CPU resources when idle?
Hardly. CPU usage only goes up while the AI is answering or while media is being processed, and the AI model is unloaded from memory after a while without use.

---

### Q7. How much RAM does Offlined need?
The setup assistant recommends the AI model that best fits your computer's memory: small models such as **Qwen 3.5 4B** run with 8 GB of RAM, while larger ones such as **GPT-OSS 20B** need about 16 GB.
The application itself is lightweight; memory use depends mainly on the AI model you choose.

---

### Q8. Is offline Wikipedia required?
No. Wikipedia is optional, but recommended: the AI uses it to cite its sources.

---

### Q9. Does Offlined work on Linux or macOS?
Windows x64 is the supported platform today.
macOS and Linux support are planned.

---

### Q10. How do I move my data to a new version?
Your data is stored in the `Offlined Data` folder, next to the app folder (it is created the first time you run Offlined).

Copy that folder next to the new version of Offlined to keep your downloads, documents and media.

---

### Q11. What happens if I delete a file from the Library?
Deleted files go to a recycle bin first, so you can recover them if you delete something by mistake.

---

### Q12. Can I use Offlined at work, at school or in my organization?
Offlined is **free for personal use**. Use by or for a company, a freelancer's business, a school, a public administration, an NGO or any other organization requires a **professional license**. Write to **offlined_os@proton.me** and we will get back to you.
